# The pupil's own text is readable only by the pupil

Word cards and any other text a pupil writes in their own words are kept in their progress like a notebook, but are never sent to teachers', guardians' or classmates' screens, the leaderboard or logs; they leave the pupil's account only for their own syncing, their export and grading they ask for. Guardians see the list of hard words, not the text. Letting a guardian or teacher read the cards would be a deliberate, separate decision.
