# Fallaskóli

Self-paced Icelandic grammar and vocabulary practice for pupils in years 8–10. It is
being extended with a word track, sentence and text comprehension and story telling,
designed so that pupils with developmental language disorder benefit without anyone
being singled out.

## People

**Pupil** (nemandi):
A child who practises. Never shown a label about why a question was chosen for them.
_Avoid_: student, user, learner

**Administrator**:
The person who runs the website and sets each pupil's ceiling, including whether server-graded questions are on.
_Avoid_: admin user, owner (the owner is the person who decides what the program is)

**Teacher**:
Sees a group's patterns and assignments and switches group features such as Ritun vikunnar. Never sees a pupil's own text in their progress.

**Guardian**:
A pupil's parent or carer who may see a summary of that pupil's progress, such as the list of hard words.

## Practice

**Skill** (flokkur):
One of the categories on the Færnikort (Nafnorð, Setningagerð, Námsorð …); XP, levels and þyngd are kept per skill.
_Avoid_: category, topic, subject

**Question type**:
One kind of question, such as "put the noun in the right case"; it belongs to exactly one skill.
_Avoid_: exercise, task type, item

**Þyngd**:
How hard the questions in a skill are for this pupil right now, on a 1–7 scale that rises and falls with the pupil's answers.
_Avoid_: difficulty, level (level is earned by XP)

**Lota dagsins**:
The day's suggested practice session of ten questions across skills.
_Avoid_: daily quiz

**Recall**:
Producing a word oneself from its meaning or a sentence gap, without seeing it. Typing an inflected form of a word that is shown is not recall.

**Recognition**:
Choosing the right word or meaning from options that are shown.

**Server-graded question**:
A question whose answer is read on the server (by Claude) before it can be judged. It exists only for pupils the administrator has switched on, and only on the website.
_Avoid_: LLM question, AI question

**Not judged**:
The outcome of a server-graded question when the grader could not answer: the pupil gets credit for finishing, and the answer counts as neither right nor wrong.

## Words

**Word**:
A lemma with its word class, such as *hestur* (masculine noun); the same lemma in another class is another word.
_Avoid_: term, item, vocabulary item

**Word entry**:
One record that a pupil answered one question about one word on one day: which question type, and right, wrong or seen-not-judged. A table of ten boxes about one word is one entry.
_Avoid_: attempt, log line

**Word knowledge**:
Everything the program knows about how one pupil stands with each word, worked out from their word entries.
_Avoid_: word log, vocab record

**Word in learning** (shown as *Orðin mín*):
One of the six LÍNO-2 words a pupil is currently learning intensively.
_Avoid_: target word, focus word, DLD word

**Learned word**:
A word in learning that has been answered right in at least four question types, one of them recall, on at least two days, with the last three answers right. It then joins the review queue.
_Avoid_: mastered word, known word

**Hard word**:
Any word a pupil has answered wrong three times across question types within fourteen days.
_Avoid_: problem word, weak word

**Word health**:
How a word the pupil has met is going recently: new, weak, shaky, ok or strong.

**Rescue drill**:
A short run of questions on one weak word, offered to the pupil.

**Review queue**:
What a pupil missed, brought back after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days.
_Avoid_: spaced repetition list

**Þrjár í röð**:
Three questions straight away on something the pupil just got wrong, offered and never imposed.

## Progress and privacy

**Progress**:
Everything saved about one pupil's practice, kept in the browser and on the server and merged between their devices.
_Avoid_: save file, account data

**Pupil's own text**:
Text the pupil wrote in their own words (word cards). Only the pupil can read it; it leaves their account only for their own syncing, their export and grading they ask for.
_Avoid_: user content, submissions

**Word card** (Orðakort):
The pupil's notebook page for a word in learning: their own explanation, their own sentence and related words.

## Content

**Text**:
A short story (saga) or subject text (fræðitexti) written for comprehension questions, marked with its story-map parts or main idea and its pronoun references.
_Avoid_: passage, article, reading

**Story map**:
The six parts of an episode used throughout the program: aðstæður, kveikja/vandi, markmið, tilraun, útkoma, viðbrögð/tilfinning.
